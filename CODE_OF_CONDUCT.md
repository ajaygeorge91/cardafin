# Code of conduct

This is a small project with one maintainer, so this is short and describes
what actually happens rather than a process that does not exist.

## Expected

Be straightforward and civil. Critique code, not people. Assume the person on
the other end is competent and busy. Disagreement about a technical decision is
welcome and useful; keep it about the decision.

If you are new to Cardano, CBOR or any of the rest of it, ask. "I don't
understand why this hashes to 28 bytes" is a good question, and the answer
probably belongs in a comment.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexual attention,
publishing anyone's private information, or sustained disruption of a
discussion. Sockpuppeting or persistently reopening a settled decision to wear
people down.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages in this repository.

## Enforcement

Report it to the maintainer (see the contact in the repository profile, or use
[GitHub's report-abuse form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) if you
would rather go through GitHub).

I read reports, decide, and act. The options available are editing or deleting
a comment, closing a thread, and blocking an account from the repository. I
will say what I did; I will not publish the details of a report.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
without its escalation ladder, because there is no committee behind it.
